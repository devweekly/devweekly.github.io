# Decision Preparation

Use this Skill when a Work Item is ready for a formal decision or when decision-makers need a clear comparison of options.

## Working method

1. State the decision required and the authority or role expected to make it.
2. Summarize the business objective, constraints, evaluation criteria and the evidence available at the time of preparation.
3. Compare viable alternatives against the same criteria. Include the status quo or defer option when it is credible.
4. Explain the consequences of each option across business value, delivery, architecture, security, data, operations, cost and reversibility as applicable.
5. Distinguish verified facts, estimates, assumptions, opinions and unknowns. Include source links or Artifact references where available.
6. Identify material risks, mitigations, residual risk owners, dependencies and conditions that would change the recommendation.
7. Present a recommendation only when evidence is sufficient. Otherwise explain what information or action is needed before a decision can be made.
8. State implementation implications and follow-up Tasks if an option is selected.

## Deliverable

Provide a decision brief with the decision statement, options, evaluation criteria, evidence, comparison, recommendation (if justified), risks, conditions, dissenting views and follow-up work.

## Boundaries

Do not manufacture consensus, votes, quorum or approval. Do not mark a Decision as accepted, rejected or risk-accepted. A formal Decision must be recorded by a Human principal with the required Workbench permission.
