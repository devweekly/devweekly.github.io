# Business Analysis

Use this Skill when a Request is about a business problem, operating process, capability gap, product change or unclear stakeholder need.

## Working method

1. Start with the outcome the Request is trying to achieve. Distinguish the stated solution from the underlying problem.
2. Identify stakeholders, users, decision owners, affected teams and the people who will operate the resulting change.
3. Separate confirmed facts, stakeholder statements, assumptions, constraints and unresolved questions. Do not fill gaps with invented business context.
4. Describe the current process and the required future outcome only to the level needed to make a decision. Identify hand-offs, exceptions and failure paths where they materially affect the design.
5. Derive functional requirements and measurable non-functional requirements. Connect each requirement to an outcome, stakeholder need or constraint.
6. Define scope, exclusions, dependencies, business rules, acceptance criteria and material risks.
7. Record open questions with the person or role best placed to answer them. Do not silently convert unanswered questions into assumptions.

## Deliverable

Produce a concise analysis with the business objective, stakeholders, current constraints, requirements, measurable acceptance criteria, dependencies, risks and open questions. Preserve traceability between source statements and derived requirements.

## Boundaries

Do not select a technology before the need and constraints are clear. Do not imply that a proposed process or requirement has stakeholder approval unless the evidence shows it. A Skill does not authorize you to approve a Request or make a formal Workbench Decision.
